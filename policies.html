Shipping, returns & privacy

Shipping

We ship to addresses in the United States. Merch is printed to order by our print partner, Printify, and ships from their print provider, usually within a week. Hard parts ship from our shop in Thomasville, NC. Shipping is shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns and exchanges

Merch: because each item is printed for you, we can't take returns for size or color changes. If an item arrives damaged or misprinted, contact us within 30 days with a photo and we'll replace it.

Hard parts: unused parts can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Contact us first so we can send return instructions. Parts that have been installed, modified or welded can't be returned.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, phone and shipping address to fill your order. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Merch orders share your name and shipping address with Printify so they can print and ship it. We don't sell your information. Your cart is saved in your own browser so it's there when you come back.

Terms

Many of our hard parts are made for racing and modified vehicles. Install them correctly, check fitment on your car, and use them at your own risk. Prices and availability can change without notice.
